Method

Gradient boosting, one model per horizon, trained on EPA AirData hourly NO2 for 2024 and checked on all of 2025. At 12 hours its mean absolute error was 3.82 ppb, 33% lower than assuming the next reading equals this one.

Intervals are split-conformal: on held-out data, 90% of actual readings fell inside them.

Live readings are preliminary and not yet quality-assured by EPA. This is a research forecast, not a health advisory.
